---
name: Event voucher activation window
description: Chef-event vouchers require a first paid purchase within 30 days to retain their original 12-month validity.
---

Chef-event vouchers use a 30-day activation deadline. A successful purchase using the code records activation; after the deadline, only activated codes remain valid, and all codes still expire at their original 12-month date.

**Why:** The promotion needs a short activation window without shortening the promised 12-month benefit for participants who use the code.

**How to apply:** Keep validation centralized, record activation atomically with successful payment, and keep the QR card, registration page, and voucher email consistent with the 30-day/12-month rule.
