For French product cards, keep product-image dimensions unchanged and align prices and discount badges across regular-price and sale cards.

**Why:** This is an explicit project requirement. The live French grid showed sale badges shifting prices upward relative to regular-price cards.

**How to apply:** When changing tax-label or sale-price layout, preserve the existing image height and reserve a stable discount row across cards. Check both the homepage carousel and product grid.
