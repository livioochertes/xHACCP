name: Metro customer estimate
description: Business context and truthfulness constraints for projected customer counts based on Metro Romania acquisitions.
---

Metro Romania brings roughly 3,500 new customers per year, with acquisitions concentrated more heavily in spring and autumn. The user approved an estimated public counter, not a live count of individual customers.

**Why:** A flat daily increase is only an approximation and does not represent the seasonal pattern or confirm that every person is satisfied.

**How to apply:** Keep projected values clearly labeled as estimates. Do not describe them as live, confirmed, or actual customer counts unless an authoritative Metro data feed is connected.
