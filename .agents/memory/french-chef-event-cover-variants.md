This campaign has distinct light and navy cover variants. If a reference to “the other cover” is ambiguous, compare the selected canvas visual or ask before exporting; the intended cover for the current homepage adaptation is the light version shown in the selected frame.

Keep the original high-resolution group photo as a separate source asset and render both covers from it; do not upscale the small thumbnail for 2× exports.

**Why:** Both variants share the event photo, French copy, and similar export dimensions, so assuming the color variant from relative wording led to attaching the wrong files. Enlarging the small thumbnail also made the mobile photo look soft.

**How to apply:** Before preparing or replacing campaign exports, verify the light/navy variant against the selected visual, keep the original photo intact, and check sharpness at the final 2× dimensions.
